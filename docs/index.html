Big logs

Gigabytes open instantly. Then cut them down.

Show, hide or mark lines with filters combined by AND, OR and NOT. The timeline shows error bursts at a glance; select a range to zoom in.

  • Live tail through rotation and truncation
  • Several searches at once, each in its own color
  • Columns parsed for you; JSON and CSV too
Requests

Follow one request through every log.

Right-click a trace or request ID: its lines in order, across threads, open logs and whole folders, inside zips too, with the longest wait marked.

  • Search folders in parallel, inside .gz and .zip
  • Merge logs by time; compare a bad run with a good one
  • Remote logs over SSH, followed live
Errors

Thousands of exceptions, a handful of causes.

Java, .NET and Python stack traces grouped by what went wrong and where in your code. Click a frame to open it in your editor.

  • Known problems pointed out, with what to try
  • Durations and numbers in messages, charted
  • Insights: bursts, restarts and slow spots
The JVM

Threads, memory and CPU, explained.

Open thread dumps or attach to a running JVM, also over SSH. Deadlocks, lock queues and stuck threads are pointed out; heap dumps say what fills the memory and why it's still alive.

  • Flame graphs of CPU, allocations and locks
  • GC logs: the pauses, and why
  • Crash dumps and hs_err reports in plain words
